# Phase 1 product truth

Kandamkulathi Digital Commerce & Companion serves customers, authorized clinic staff and assigned physicians. The existing repository and reviewed Phase 1 plan are the product authority; this file does not add scope or claim acceptance.

## Current implementation objective

- Deliver real persisted core shopping/payment and doctor/booking journeys through web and native clients.
- Enforce identity, ownership, clinic scope, consent and required staff MFA on the server.
- Provide bounded staff read views, controlled knowledge editing and a cited approved-content Companion foundation.
- Preserve atomic stock, booking, integer-paise ledger and outbox invariants.
- Verify the integrated candidate with unit, real PostgreSQL, HTTP, browser, security and applicable device/provider evidence.

## Boundaries

Missing company resources, approved clinical/financial policies, provider eligibility, signing credentials or physical-device evidence remain explicit blockers. Synthetic examples belong in tests or visibly identified development fixtures; they cannot establish company approval or production success. Do not invent medical advice, prices, tax/refund rules, company testimonials or provider receipts.

Phase 1 is an internally verified foundation, not public release. Full feature coverage and public-channel/production acceptance retain their later phase gates. A passing local test does not make G1 Passed.

## Existing interface authority

Extend the existing public, workspace and native surfaces without a visual rebrand. Preserve the incumbent green/neutral palette, system and bundled Malayalam/Tamil type, native semantics, language selection and session-state vocabulary. Prioritize legible workflows, explicit confirmation and safe recovery over decoration. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
