# Release and rollback runbook

**Release manager:** Devadathan. **Technical backup/clinical lead:** Mathew. **Commerce reconciliation:** Josin. **QA report:** Tinu. Company actual product/clinical/finance/operations signatories and incident contacts must be assigned. No live rollout/approval is executed by this file.

## 1. Assemble candidate

Bind R/output/package/PR and all due canonical test rows. Pin SHA/tag/API/web/native/schema/config/provider/model/prompt/KB and dependency versions; confirm current approved company input, migration/backfill/old-client compatibility and provider/store/listing readiness. Freeze unrelated integration changes. Use new candidate IDs after fixes with impacted evidence invalidated.

## 2. Verify phase/gate

G1 current foundation; G2 full required function and complete signed native candidates; G3 paid measured configuration, cumulative functional/security/clinical/device/provider/load/soak/recovery, company UAT/training and actual public prerequisites. No confirmed critical/high or safety/privacy/financial/acceptance blocker. Submit complete tested native apps after D7 paid regression; internal/submitted/pending approval is not final public delivery.

G1/G2 Pass permits reviewed regular dev->main milestone promotion only. Verify source/tree/artifact equivalence and critical postmerge smoke; no automatic public deploy. For final release company/technical reviewers record prelaunch Go/No-go using the [gate report](../quality/GATE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md). Missing due scope/approval/evidence means No-go and revised forecast, not a waived requirement.

## 3. Controlled production change after Go

Verify actual target/account/resource/provider mode, approved opening data/balances and backup/restore/keys/object manifests. Review migration IDs/order/lock impact and compatible rollback artifact. One authorized operator executes additive migration/backfill and tested backend/web artifacts in the approved order; keep supported native API compatibility. Actual commands must be in the implemented command registry before use; none are fabricated here.

Start with minimal approved safe company accounts/recipients. Verify required web/native/WhatsApp purchase/booking/private consultation/confirmed tool, jobs/notices/invoices and identity/current-state behavior. Reconcile provider/order/payment/refund/stock/booking/outbox/credits. Observe declared metrics/alerts for the agreed recorded window and check actual public Android/iOS/listing/provider access.

## 4. Stop/rollback triggers

Stop widening release and notify the named incident lead for any unauthorized/private/clinical unsafe effect, duplicate money/oversell/doctor overlap, untrusted paid state, failed reconciliation/recovery or required-channel loss. Compare latency/errors/pool/backlog and business deadlines to accepted workload/production thresholds; the company records exact alert/observation windows before Go. Never keep a harmful path open to preserve a cheap budget or deadline.

Contain; execute prior schema-compatible Worker/web rollback by approved operator if indicated; preserve evidence and reconcile in-flight provider/job state before retry. Target <=15-minute recovery-to-service must have rehearsal evidence. A destructive down migration or instant app-store rollback is not assumed. Follow [incident response](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md) and [recovery](BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md).

## 5. Final closure

Close G3/tag only after safe postdeploy observation/control totals, actual public required channels, all 38 company acceptances, recorded role training, company service/source/billing/signing/recovery ownership and support/maintenance handover pass. Record any allowed low-risk residual owner/date. Synchronize approved main/hotfix history back to dev. All result fields remain Pending until executed/signed.
