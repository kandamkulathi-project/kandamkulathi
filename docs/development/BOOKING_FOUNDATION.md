# Booking foundation — KAN-85 / P1-MAT-002

**Status: bounded implementation; package and phase acceptance Pending.** This adds strict reusable contracts and pure decisions, not a booking API, persisted hold, payment integration, clinical service or concurrency proof. It follows [architecture](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md), [transaction requirements](../architecture/adr/0002-domain-transactions.md), [data invariants](../architecture/DATA_MODEL.md) and [Mathew's package](../implementation/Kandamkulathi_05_Mathew_All_Phases.md#p1-mat-002--booking-doctor-availability-and-clinical-foundation). Traceability: P1-E1-003/P1-E1-004; R03/R10/R11/R12/R14/R15/R32/R36; P1-O02/P1-O03/P1-O05/P1-O06. These references do not claim those requirements are complete.

## Consumer boundaries

- `@kandamkulathi/contracts/booking`: strict versioned directory approval metadata, concrete UTC availability windows, clinic IANA zones, global leave, modality, buffers, slots, integer INR paise fee snapshots, owner, provenance, held/confirmed/expired/cancelled states and captured-payment records. Unknown keys fail validation. UTC requires canonical milliseconds (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ`); invalid calendar dates, numeric offsets and ambiguous local dates fail. Timezones are checked against the runtime IANA database; production Workers compatibility remains to be verified.
- `@kandamkulathi/domain/booking`: `decideBookingHold`, `decideBookingTransition`, `intervalsOverlap`, `bufferedInterval`, intent types and explicit transaction requirements. Malformed contracts throw validation errors; valid but disallowed decisions return typed `rejected` reasons. A future HTTP adapter must map validation errors safely, without echoing private payloads.
- `holdBookingRequestSchema` has no actor, fee, expiry, duration or end-time input. Caller-supplied owner/location/version identify the requested resource, not permission. Current identity, organization/MFA, clinic scope, time, availability, leave, occupied intervals, approved fee and policy must come from trusted server reads. Parsing a `bookingAuthoritySchema` object does **not** authenticate it. Never construct authority or a verified payment from client JSON or actor headers.
- All internal UUIDs use the shared `canonicalUuidSchema` from `packages/contracts/src/identifiers.ts`: validated UUIDs normalize to lowercase before ownership, clinic-scope, payment-binding, leave and doctor-conflict comparisons. Casing cannot create a second identity or bypass a global conflict. Opaque provider order/payment/event identifiers are not UUIDs and remain case-sensitive; do not lowercase them. Mixed-case regressions cover both request and stored-record casing directions.
- Every accepted result is a proposal with `persisted: false`. No production adapter exists, and no API may present an intent as a stored/confirmed appointment. There are no network calls, locks, migrations, provider SDKs or production in-memory persistence in this package.

## Scheduling and provenance

Availability is a concrete versioned UTC window, not a recurrence expansion engine. Slot end and grid alignment derive from its explicit slot length. IANA clinic timezones describe presentation; no implicit local-time or DST conversion occurs. Recurrence expansion and ambiguous/nonexistent wall-time policy require reviewed future integration. Hold duration, slot length, buffers and fee are injected values; test numbers are synthetic, not business defaults. Holds extending past slot start are rejected rather than silently shortened.

Overlap uses `[start - beforeBuffer, end + afterBuffer)` globally per doctor, never per clinic or modality. Exact buffered adjacency works; a one-millisecond overlap does not. Global leave is checked against the expanded proposed interval. The occupied snapshot must contain all relevant doctor-global held/confirmed rows; it must not be clinic-filtered. An elapsed hold remains an exclusion conflict until its explicit expiry transition commits. Cancelled/expired rows do not occupy a slot. Invalid/overflowing expanded intervals fail closed.

Customer-origin bookings record the authenticated owner as `online` provenance (the selected consultation modality may be online or in-clinic). Authorized front desk can propose only `in_clinic` appointments in its current verified clinic scope, recording `front_desk` and the staff identifier. Both sources share the same owner, fee snapshot, hold, payment, expiry and cancellation lifecycle. No product-order path or administrator bypass is introduced. A fee snapshot is copied and deeply frozen in the intent; transitions preserve it rather than repricing from a client or current catalogue.

## Transition and payment rules

Every transition requires explicit expected revision, expected owner, current server clock, current authority and reviewed policy. Only payment-service authority matching the verification receipt may submit a captured payment; only expiry-service authority may expire a hold. Cancellation additionally requires an explicit current policy permission and current customer-owner or front-desk clinic scope. There is no default cancellation/refund amount or medical policy.

- Before confirmation, the adapter must verify provider authenticity **and** reconcile captured status, order/booking binding, amount and currency. A verification-reference field alone is not evidence. No provider verification is implemented here.
- An exact captured payment before expiry can propose confirmation. A server time exactly at the hold deadline is late, even if the provider event claims an earlier timestamp. A changed policy, wrong amount/order, late capture, cancelled booking or additional capture produces a typed `rebook_or_refund_required` obligation with stable business operation ID and `pending_review_and_reconciliation` status. It does not select a replacement slot, calculate a refund, settle one or reconfirm a terminal booking.
- Duplicate captured payment business identity with unchanged authoritative facts is an authorized no-op, including different delivery IDs and stale original expected revision. Conflicting facts or reused event identity for another payment are rejected. New payments with stale revisions are rejected for a current-state retry; they must not be acknowledged as recorded. Replay never adds a second applied payment or duplicate obligation.
- Policy changes do not prevent explicit expiry at the immutable hold deadline. A mismatched current policy cannot authorize cancellation/confirmation. Confirmation preserves a still-held interval; new leave/schedule changes and their effect on existing holds need reviewed operational policy before routes are enabled.
- Confirmed cancellation retains the original captured-payment audit and proposes reconciliation; no refund success is asserted. Failed/unknown provider outcomes and unknown database commit outcomes require durable reconciliation before retry. Replayed decisions alone are not a durable idempotency mechanism.

## Required persistence integration (not implemented)

Platform PR3 remains unmerged and owns the selected driver/migrations. A future reviewed adapter must:

1. Use actual PostgreSQL 17, least-privilege non-owner runtime identity and transaction-local RLS context. Re-read current owner, identity/revocation/scope, policy, schedule/leave and fee under the real transaction path; re-evaluate proposals there.
2. Add reviewed doctor-global half-open GiST/btree_gist exclusion including both buffers on active held/confirmed intervals. Do not include clinic in the exclusion identity. Explicitly expire stale rows atomically before release; application snapshot checks alone cannot prevent a race.
3. Enforce CAS by booking ID, expected owner and revision with checked affected-row counts, immutable snapshot/audit history, unique provider payment business identities and delivery records. Retain/redeliver alias event IDs safely; a no-op cannot replace a durable event registry.
4. Commit state, ledger/payment reconciliation, audit and durable outbox together; preserve obligations across cancellation and retries. Uniquely key outbox operations and keep rebook/refund fulfillment under independently reviewed payment/refund policy. Capture mismatches must be durably routed, not discarded. Reconcile rejected/unmatched callbacks without leaking another booking.
5. Perform provider work only after commit, outside locks. Prove bounded transaction retries, crash/replay/unknown-commit recovery, no double-confirmation, no double-refund, control totals and pool/RLS cleanup on the selected driver.

No fake repository or guessed SQL migration is supplied. The local Docker daemon/real PostgreSQL prerequisite is unavailable, so real exclusion, transaction, RLS, outbox and concurrency integration tests are **Blocked**, not passed by fixtures.

## Clinical boundary

`bookingClinicalFoundationSchema` accepts only `unavailable / REVIEWED_CLINICAL_POLICY_REQUIRED`. Notes, recommendations and dose schedule payloads are deliberately unavailable. No dosage, consent/guardian/retention policy, patient-history permission or signing policy is fabricated. P1-MAT-001 owns reviewed identity/clinical access integration. Physician/company approval and independently reviewed assignment, identity, consultation consent and permitted past-purchase access remain required before private clinical routes or signed recommendation payloads are enabled.
